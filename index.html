NATIONWIDE SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

1. Shipping Rates & Free Delivery

We offer flat-rate standard shipping of ₨250 across all cities and regions of Pakistan. Orders exceeding ₨5,000 qualify automatically for 100% FREE nationwide delivery.

2. Estimated Delivery Timelines

  • Lahore & Karachi Express: 1 to 2 business days.
  • Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sialkot: 2 to 3 business days.
  • Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Hyderabad & other cities: 3 to 4 business days.
  • Remote & Northern Territories: 4 to 6 business days.

3. Courier Partners & Tracking

All consignments are dispatched exclusively through verified tier-1 logistics partners including TCS Express, Call Courier, Trax Logistics, and Leopard Courier. You will receive an automated tracking link via SMS and WhatsApp as soon as your parcel is scanned at the departure hub.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Inspection

Per courier safety regulations in Pakistan, consignments must be paid to the courier rider prior to opening the flyer. Once received, our 7-day hassle-free exchange policy guarantees immediate replacement if any sizing or defect occurs.

PEACE OF MIND GUARANTEE

7-DAY EXCHANGE & RETURNS POLICY

At Brand Collection, customer satisfaction is paramount. If you are not completely satisfied with the fit or finish of your garment, we provide an effortless 7-day exchange process.

Exchange Conditions

  • Item must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags and flyer intact.
  • Exchange request must be initiated within 7 calendar days of delivery.
  • Free size exchanges are provided for all regular priced apparel.

How to Initiate an Exchange

  1. Message our WhatsApp concierge at +966 54 380 5017 with your Order Number (e.g. BC-89241).
  2. State the replacement size or design you prefer.
  3. Our courier partner will arrange reverse pickup directly from your doorstep in major Pakistani cities.

Refunds & Store Credit

In the rare event of a manufacturing defect where an exchange is unavailable, refunds are processed via direct IBFT bank transfer to any Pakistani bank account or Easypaisa/JazzCash within 3 business days.
